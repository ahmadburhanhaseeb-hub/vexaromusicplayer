Key Features :

_Gesture-Based Controls :_

_Play / Pause_ : Tap directly on the top main album art cover.

_Next Song_ : Swipe the album art forward.

_Previous Song_ : Swipe the album art in reverse.
_Add to Queue_ : Slide any music in the list from left to right

Smart Navigation Shortcuts:

_Lyrics_ : Press and hold the artist title and song title for two seconds.

_Search Screen_ : Double-tap on the title area.

_Quick Access Icons_ : Switch between views using the built-in toolbar icons:

_Folder Preview_ : Browse your local music files by folders.

_Queue_ : Manage and view your upcoming playlist queue.
